Why Turnbow Consulting

Led by a customer feedback expert with a decade in the trenches.

Turnbow Consulting was founded by Cam Turnbow, one of the first people to build a career specifically around win-loss research. Cam joined Clozd — one of the category's founding firms — as its 8th employee back in 2018, and has since personally conducted more than 2,200 customer interviews, spanning companies from the Fortune 100 down to early-stage startups, across nearly every industry.

Before Clozd, Cam ran the voice-of-customer program at Instructure, where he saw firsthand just how valuable unfiltered customer feedback is — and how rarely most companies actually get it. Turnbow Consulting exists to close that gap — bringing the same rigor and independence to companies of any size, without the enterprise price tag.

Step 1 — The Introduction Email

To secure the interview, we ask you to send a brief email to your customer explaining that your company uses an independent third party — Turnbow Consulting — to gather honest feedback about their experience.

Turnbow Consulting is CC'd on this email and takes over from there: sharing the scheduling link, answering questions, and following up as needed so the interview gets on the calendar.

Once scheduled: the call is recorded, and the recording, transcript, and summary are delivered directly to you and your team.

To: customer@example.com
Cc: cam@turnbowconsulting.com
Subject: Quick favor — 30 min for honest feedback?
Hi [Customer Name],

We're always working to improve, and we'd love to hear your honest take on working with us. To make sure you can speak freely, we've partnered with Turnbow Consulting, an independent qualitative research firm, to conduct a short 30-minute conversation on our behalf.

I've cc'd their team and included a scheduling link to find a time that works for you. Thanks in advance for sharing your perspective!

How do I pay?+

Yes. Customers have 6 months from the date of purchase to use the interviews in their bundle. No refunds are given for unused interviews.
